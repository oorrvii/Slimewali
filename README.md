# Slimewali

Handmade slime, shipped across India.

**Live site:** [slimewali.vercel.app](https://slimewali.vercel.app/)

Slimewali is a small online shop. A customer picks products and sends an order request. The owner reviews it, arranges payment personally, and then packs and ships the order. Every step is tracked and visible to the customer.

## Features

**For customers**
- Browse and search slimes by category, price, stock and discounts
- Product pages with variants and stock status
- Cart, checkout, and sign-in with Google
- An account with order history, live order status and tracking, and saved addresses

**For the owner**
- Order review, approval, payment confirmation, dispatch and delivery tracking
- Product, category and stock management with image upload
- Shop settings, sale banner, discounts and editable pages (About, FAQ, Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms)
- Ready-made customer messages for each order stage
- New-order email alerts and a downloadable order backup

## Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Firebase (sign-in and database), Cloudinary (images) and Vercel (hosting).
